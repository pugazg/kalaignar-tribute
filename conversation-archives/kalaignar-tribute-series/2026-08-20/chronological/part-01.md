Three major influences.

Periyar argued:

- Caste inequalities are real.

> First acknowledge differences.

Only then can equality emerge.

### 2. Anna

Anna took Periyar's social analysis and applied it to Indian federalism.

His argument was:

Tamil identity and Indian identity need not be enemies.

A Tamil can be:

- Tamil by language
- Dravidian by culture
- Indian by nationality

These identities can coexist.

Anna famously transformed the movement from demanding Dravida Nadu to demanding stronger state rights within India.

Kalaignar inherited that framework.
