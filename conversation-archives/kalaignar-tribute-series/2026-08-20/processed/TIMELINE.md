# Kalaignar Tribute Series — Timeline

## Beginning of the Series

The archive begins with a plan to collect and preserve tribute messages, speeches, memories and personal recollections about Dr. Kalaignar.

The first recorded material focuses on tribute speech transcripts and extracting meaningful human moments from them rather than only preserving raw transcripts.

## Early Archive Direction

The conversations established a pattern:

1. Capture the original tribute source.
2. Preserve transcript-level material.
3. Identify emotionally significant and historically useful sections.
4. Prepare structured content suitable for public remembrance.

## Archival Evolution

The project gradually moved from individual tribute posts into a broader digital preservation approach:

- conversation archives
- source preservation
- structured documentation
- reusable workflows

## Current Processing Activity

The 2026-08-20 chronological archive is being transformed into a curated knowledge layer while keeping the original conversation parts unchanged.
